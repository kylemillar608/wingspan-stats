📱 Add this site to your phone’s home screen

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow). On newer iOS versions it’s inside the ••• menu.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

In Chrome on iPhone, tap the Share icon in the address bar, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.

The shortcut opens full-screen with the bird icon. Use the ↻ Refresh button to fetch new games.
